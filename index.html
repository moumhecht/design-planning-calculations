Qubit–Resonator Capacitance Solver

Solves for CQ, CR, Cg, and inductance L given target qubit/resonator frequencies, qubit charging energy, and coupling strength g. The qubit's EJ/EC ratio is derived from fQ and ECQ rather than fixed, and L is constrained to a physically realizable on-chip range.

Parameters
fQ target (GHz) 4.50
fR target (GHz) 5.75
ECQ target (MHz) 200
g target (MHz) −50
L is bounded to [0.1, 5] nH (fabricated Xmon/resonator inductors). fR should exceed fQ.
